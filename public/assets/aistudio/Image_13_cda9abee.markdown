## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an exacting, broadcast-grade laboratory environment tailored for founders, creators, and leaders training in spontaneous oration and rhetorical agility. It rejects gamified gimmicks, juvenile avatars, and fluorescent AI gradients in favor of an austere, high-stakes acoustic studio aesthetic. 

The emotional signature is razor-sharp focus, quiet authority, and technical mastery:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep control-room darkness, reminiscent of sound-dampened recording studios and analog modular hardware translated into fluid software.
- **Visual Movement:** A deliberate synthesis of *Technical Minimalist* architecture and *Modern Editorial* structure. High-contrast typography commands the layout, surrounded by generous negative space and micro-meter precision.
- **Tone:** Methodical, premium, and calm under cognitive load. Visual noise is stripped down so the user’s mind and voice remain the centerpiece.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around spectral precision against an ultra-deep charcoal backdrop, delivering maximum legibility and surgical accents without ocular fatigue.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#090B10` — The foundational, near-black vacuum setting the tone of a dark studio.
- **Surface Level 1 (Recessed/Muted):** `#11151D` — Used for backplanes, inactive troughs, and secondary panes.
- **Surface Level 2 (Default Container):** `#161B26` — Default elevated cards, analysis trays, and studio modules.
- **Surface Level 3 (Active/Interactive Surface):** `#1E2433` — Hovered containers, segmented controls, and raised hardware-like tactile panels.

### Structural Lines & Separators
- **Subtle Hairline:** `#252B38` — 1px division rules, muted structural partitions, and neutral card outlines.
- **Active Structural Rim:** `#2E3646` — Interactive component outlines, focused container borders, and structural grips.

### Typographic Tones
- **Crisp Primary:** `#F4F6FB` — Editorial headers, live telemetry readouts, active prompts.
- **Cool Muted Secondary:** `#969FB0` — Subheaders, contextual prompts, field labels.
- **Subtle Tertiary:** `#5A6478` — Structural metadata, keyboard shortcuts, disabled markers.

### Signal & Diagnostic Accents
- **Primary Electric Blue:** `#6C7CFF` — Primary action drivers, interactive glows, and recording states.
- **Secondary Violet:** `#A78BFA` — Rhetorical feedback tags, cadence insights, and cognitive metrics.
- **Cyan Highlight:** `#38BDF8` — Pitch modulation monitors, technical metrics, and timeline ticks.
- **Success Mint:** `#10B981` — Fluency mastery, target duration achieved, clean articulation markers.
- **Warning Amber:** `#F59E0B` — Filler word spikes, pace drift, and time running out.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges expressive rhetorical theater with precision instrument diagnostics:

- **Display & Headings (Syne):** Reserved for striking prompt delivery, core topic cues, and high-impact editorial moments. Its sculpted structural geometry conveys tension and intention.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean readability under speed. Modest optical contrast ensures rapid perception during high-adrenaline recording sessions.
- **Telemetry & Labels (JetBrains Mono):** Mandatory for all metrics, pace monitors (WPM), live decibel meters, countdowns, timestamps, and architectural tags. Tabular figures prevent layout shifts during live audio capture.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, engineered for thumb-reach operational ergonomics and zero-latency access during live speaking drills.

### Grid & Canvas Mechanics
- **Mobile (Base to 639px):** 4-column fluid layout with dynamic vertical staging. Fixed dock area reserved for audio triggers, prompt cycling, and live input indicators.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px):** 8-column layout. Split-pane configuration balancing live teleprompter/drills on the primary canvas and real-time waveform telemetry on the secondary pane.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fixed-max layout (1280px max-width container). Centered focus zone for uninterrupted vocal performance drills, bracketed by persistent diagnostic strips.

### Spatial Rhythm
Strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm creates predictable vertical scanning. Spacing within recording views prioritizes wide fields of negative space around active prompt words to reduce visual crowding, while diagnostic drawers use high-density, compact metric grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through disciplined surface layering and micro-luminance rather than heavy dropped shadows. The interface behaves like backlit studio hardware.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Level (`#090B10`):** The structural stage.
- **Panel Stack (`#11151D` to `#161B26`):** Modular audio bays and card structures rise above the base through surface color shifts rather than heavy black shadows.
- **Floating Controls (`#1E2433`):** Elevated tactile strips and floating studio toolbars leverage subtle top inner highlights: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Border Rims
Every container utilizes crisp 1px hairline strokes (`#252B38` or `#2E3646`). No element sits unanchored; every module is bounded like precision instrument rack units.

### Glow & Lighting
Glow effects are restrained strictly to active states:
- Active recording or listening states emit an electric blue aura: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(108, 124, 255, 0.25)`.
- Critical pace over-threshold warnings leverage an amber wash: `0 0 20px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.
- Surfaces do not use noisy skeuomorphic bevels, heavy diffuse blurs, or colored AI gradients.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is crisp, technical, and slightly softened to prevent clinical harshness while retaining a hardware feel:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to micro tags, status indicators, audio meter cells, and metric boxes.
- **Medium Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to standard action buttons, input fields, tactical switches, and telemetry chips.
- **Large Radius (0.75rem / 12px):** Applied to prompt cards, audio feedback trays, and elevated modules.
- **Full Radius (Pill):** Strictly limited to audio recording toggles, active listening indicators, and floating time pill controls.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA (Record / Begin Sprint):** Electric Blue (`#6C7CFF`) fill with Crisp Primary (`#F4F6FB`) typography. High-contrast, tactile press state (scale 0.98), paired with an ambient 16px electric blue focus halo.
- **Studio Secondary:** Surface `#161B26` with a hairline stroke `#2E3646`. Hover transitions background to `#1E2433` and stroke to `#6C7CFF`.
- **Destructive / Reset:** Ghost style with muted cool slate (`#969FB0`) transitioning to warning amber (`#F59E0B`) on press.

### Chips & Tactical Badges
- Constructed with `label-sm` or `label-md` in JetBrains Mono.
- Bound by 1px borders (`#252B38`), recessed backplane (`#11151D`), with leading colored telemetry dots (3px radius) for status identification (Mint = Flow, Amber = Hesitation, Blue = Active Prompt).

### Lists & Prompt Queues
- Divided by hairline borders (`#252B38`).
- Strict horizontal alignment: tabular item numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`) in JetBrains Mono at left, high-contrast prompt copy centered, and target duration/WPM at right.
- Inactive rows maintain 50% opacity (`#5A6478`), brightening to 100% Crisp Primary (`#F4F6FB`) when prioritized.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Hardware Switches
- Replace standard rounded form controls with tactile, machined studio toggle switches.
- Inactive track: `#11151D` with 1px `#252B38` border.
- Active toggle: Electric Blue slider thumb with an instantaneous 100ms hardware spring easing.

### Input Fields
- Deep charcoal fill (`#11151D`) with 1px border (`#252B38`).
- Focus state instantly swaps border to `#6C7CFF` with an inner shadow: `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- Placeholder text anchored in Tertiary Slate (`#5A6478`).

### Prompt & Waveform Cards
- Elevated at `#161B26` with hairline border `#252B38`.
- Features an ultra-fine internal top-edge highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- Padding is generous (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to maximize cognitive comfort during spontaneous speech synthesis.

### Specialized Studio Components
- **Live Decibel & Cadence Strip:** Compact, multi-segment audio VU meters rendered in monospace grid columns. Segments illuminate sequentially from cyan to blue, with amber headroom peaks.
- **Rhetorical Teleprompter Unit:** Centered, auto-scaling Syne display text with fading edge masks (top/bottom gradient mask to `#090B10`), removing screen boundary friction during speech.
